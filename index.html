SideScoopFor Mac and iPhone

Catch every spark

On your Mac and iPhone, synced in your own iCloud Drive. No data collected.

SideScoop is a sidebar of notes, lists and small tools that slides in from the right edge of your Mac’s screen. On iPhone, the same tabs fill the screen: see SideScoop for iPhone.

Common questions

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes. SideScoop for iPhone has Day, Notes, Stickies, To-dos and Logs, and the Pro panels, full screen with the tabs along the bottom. Pro and the trial work on both with the same Apple Account. See SideScoop for iPhone.

I installed SideScoop. Where is it?

In the menu bar, near the clock: a small window shape with a strip down its right side. It has no Dock icon. Click it and choose Show Panel, or press ⇧ ⌘ E. Opening SideScoop again from Applications also shows the panel, which helps if the icon is hidden behind the camera notch.

What’s free, and what does Pro add?

Day (today, with up to 3 bells a day), Notes, Stickies, To-dos, Clips (the last 5 things you copied), Logs, the focus timer, to-do reminders and two themes are free for good. SideScoop Pro adds Day’s other days, repeats and more bells, Tables, Snippets, a longer Clips history, Commits, Maps, Doodles, Trips, extra tabs of a kind and three more themes. The full list is under Tabs.

Will I be charged when the trial ends?

No. The 14-day trial is a separate free item, so nothing is charged and nothing renews. If you don’t buy Pro, Pro tabs turn read-only, so do Day’s other days, Clips holds 5, and nothing is deleted. Day, Notes, Stickies, To-dos, Clips and Logs keep working in every tab, extra ones included. Details are under The trial and SideScoop Pro.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on another Mac, my iPhone, or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, then choose Restore Purchases on the Pro screen (on the Mac: palette menu › SideScoop Pro…, or the SideScoop menu bar menu; on iPhone: the gear button › SideScoop Pro…). You don’t pay again.

I have a code for SideScoop Pro.

Choose Redeem Code… on the Pro screen (or, on the Mac, in the menu bar menu), and type it into Apple’s sheet. On the Mac it needs macOS 15 or later. You can also redeem it in the App Store app.

Do I pay again for new panels?

No. Panels added in later updates are part of SideScoop Pro.

Where are my notes?

In your notes folder. Click the palette button at the bottom of the tab rail (or the SideScoop menu bar icon) and choose the folder’s name under Folder to open it in Finder. See Where your notes are kept.

Can I open my notes in another app?

Yes. Notes, lists and tables are plain Markdown files, so any Markdown or text editor can open them. See Using another Markdown editor.

Can I paste a table from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Cells you copy from a spreadsheet, a document or a web page and paste into a note become a table in that note. To edit tables and merge cells, use a Tables tab (Pro).

Can SideScoop remind me at a certain time?

Yes, in Day. Type something with its time in the box at the top and end it with /r, such as “Call Mom at 3pm /r”, then press Return. At 3 PM the panel slides out with a soft sound and a banner. See Day.

Can SideScoop remind me of a to-do?

Yes, for tomorrow. Point at a to-do and click the sun next to it. The next day at 9:00 AM, the panel slides out on To-dos with a Today list of what you flagged. To pick another time, choose Reminder Time… in the menu bar menu or the palette menu. If your Mac is asleep or SideScoop isn’t running then, the list appears the next time it can. There are no other dates or times, and no notifications.

The shortcut does nothing.

Another app may already use it. Pick a different one under Shortcut in the menu bar menu, or record your own. When you record one, SideScoop tells you if macOS won’t accept it.

The panel doesn’t open at the screen edge.

Check that Show at Right Screen Edge is ticked in the menu bar menu. The edge is the right side of your rightmost display, away from the corners, and you need to hold the pointer there for a moment. While a mouse button is down, it opens only if you’re dragging something.

Does Clips keep my passwords?

It skips copies that password managers mark as private (an app marks a copy as concealed or temporary). Not every app marks them, so if you copy a password from somewhere else, delete it from the tab. The history stays on this Mac and never goes to iCloud or anywhere else.

macOS asked whether SideScoop may paste from other apps.

Clips needs this, and macOS may ask after you turn Clips on. Choose Allow. To change your answer, or to stop macOS asking, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security › Paste from Other Apps. If pasting is turned off there, the Clips tab says so and stops recording.

I deleted something by mistake.

Press ⌘ Z straight away. Deleted notes, tabs and pictures also go to the Trash, so you can put them back from there. Clips you delete or clear are gone for good.

On iPhone, tap Undo on the message that shows up, or tap twice on an empty spot in the tab. A note brought back this way returns to its place in the list.

Does SideScoop work offline?

Yes. Buying Pro, starting the trial, restoring and redeeming a code need the App Store, and a picture that a note shows from a web address needs a connection. Everything else works offline.

Which languages does it support?

English only, for now.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles purchases. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I remove SideScoop?

If your notes are in SideScoop’s own folder, copy them somewhere first (palette menu › Folder › the folder’s name opens it in Finder). Then choose Quit SideScoop in the menu bar menu and drag SideScoop from Applications to the Trash. A notes folder you chose yourself stays where it is.

Opening and closing the panel

  • Shortcut: ⇧ ⌘ E, Shift, Command and E.
  • Screen edge: push the pointer against the right edge of the screen and hold it there for a moment. If you’re dragging text or a picture, the panel opens so you can drop it in.
  • Menu bar: click the SideScoop icon and choose Show Panel.

To close it, click outside it, press Esc, or press the shortcut again. The pin at the top keeps it open while you work in another app. Drag the panel’s left edge to change its width.

In the menu bar menu you can pick another Shortcut (or Custom… to record your own), untick Show at Right Screen Edge, and tick Open at Login, which is off until you turn it on. Settings… (⌘ ,) opens the palette menu, the button at the bottom of the tab rail, with the themes, the shortcut, Reminder Time… and Alarm Sound.

Tabs

Tabs run down the left side of the panel. Click + under them for a new one; Pro panels are marked Pro there. Double-click a tab to rename it. Right-click it, or click ⋯ at the top of the panel, for its color, Rename…, Show in Finder, Move Up, Move Down, Delete Tab and a few more.

Free

  • Day: today, one row per hour, with up to 3 bells a day.
  • Notes: Markdown notes with groups, colors and search. Paste or drop screenshots in, or cells from a spreadsheet to make a table.
  • Stickies: a wall of sticky notes.
  • To-dos: checklists with sub-items and details. The sun on an item brings it back tomorrow morning.
  • Clips: the last 5 things you copied, once you turn it on.
  • Logs: timestamped lines, one file a day, plus what you finished and how long you focused that day.
  • The focus timer, floating windows for notes, the shortcut and the screen edge.
  • Two themes: Sketchbook and Native.

Free tabs are one of each kind. Extra ones made during the trial keep working after it.

SideScoop Pro IN-APP PURCHASE

  • Day: the days before and after, things that repeat, and more than 3 bells a day.
  • Tables: small tables with merged cells, copied as real tables.
  • Snippets: text and pictures you want to paste again.
  • Clips keeps any number up to 500, and Keep moves a clip into Snippets.
  • Commits: tap a card each time you do something, and see counts and streaks.
  • Maps: ideas on an endless canvas, joined by lines.
  • Doodles: draw, write and drop pictures on a page.
  • Trips: plan a trip day by day.
  • More than one tab of a kind, such as a second Notes tab.
  • Three more themes: Cat, Neon and Hamster.

Pro tabs carry a small Pro mark in the tab rail until you buy Pro.

Day

Day is today, one row per hour. Double-click an empty spot to put something at that hour, type, and press Return (Esc cancels). Or type into the box at the top, with “at” before the time: “Call Mom at 3pm”, “standup at 9:30”, “lunch at 12”. Other numbers stay words (“buy 2 eggs”). Without a time it goes in the next hour; the line under the box says where it will go. Nothing is ticked off: what’s past turns grey by itself, and a line shows the time now. The hours from midnight to 6 AM fold into one line unless something is planned then; click it to open them.

Double-click a time to change it, and the line moves into place. A time that’s already past offers the next hour, so double-click and Return moves it later. Point at a line for its bell and ⋯ (or right-click it) for Change Time, Repeat, Move to Tomorrow and Delete. Nothing moves to another day on its own.

Bells: click the bell on a line, or end what you type with /r. At that time the panel slides out with a soft sound and a banner: Snooze brings it back in 10 minutes, Done puts it away. If your Mac was asleep or SideScoop wasn’t running, a bell up to 30 minutes late still comes; older ones don’t. To turn the sound off, untick Alarm Sound in the palette menu. Free: 3 bells a day.

With Pro: ‹ › at the bottom go to the days before and after, so you can plan ahead; a line can repeat every day, on weekdays (Monday to Friday) or once a week; and there’s no limit on bells. Changing a repeat on one day changes that day only, and the message that follows offers From Now On. After the trial, what you planned stays: the repeats and bells you made keep coming, and other days can still be looked at, read-only.

Clips

Clips is off until you click Turn On in the Clips tab. From the next thing you copy, it keeps the last 5 things you copied on this Mac: text, links and pictures. Click one to copy it again, or × to delete it. Copying something that’s already there moves it to the top. With Pro, double-click the number in the top-right corner of the tab and type how many to keep, from 1 to 500.

While it records, a dot shows on the menu bar icon and on the tab. Pause and Resume are in the tab, and Pause Clips and Clear Clips… in the menu bar menu. Deleting the Clips tab deletes the history and turns Clips off.

If the trial ends without a purchase, nothing in Clips is deleted. It records again once 5 or fewer are left.

Snippets PRO

Type or paste into the box at the top, drop text or pictures in, or click Keep on a clip. Click a snippet to copy it, text or picture; what you copy from Snippets doesn’t go into Clips. Double-click its text to edit it (or a picture to see it large), and click × to delete it. A long one shows a few lines until you point at it.

Tables PRO

Type a title in the box at the top and press Return for a new table, or paste cells there. Click a cell and type; Tab moves right and Return moves down. Typing into the faint row or column at the edge adds one.

To merge cells, drag across them (or Shift-click) and click Merge, or press ⌘ M. Click a merged cell for Unmerge. The handles beside rows and columns delete or move them.

Copy on the card puts the table on the clipboard, so it pastes as a table into a word processor or spreadsheet. Copy as Markdown is in the card’s menu.

The focus timer

Click the clock above the palette button, at the bottom of the tab rail, and 25 minutes start. The menu bar shows the time left, so you can close the panel. Click the clock again to stop early.

To change the length, double-click the number under the clock, type a number of minutes from 1 to 180, and press Return. The next click on the clock starts that many minutes.

When time’s up, the panel slides in with a soft sound and a Time’s up banner. Choose Again for another round or Done. Logs keeps the day’s total. To turn the sound off, untick Alarm Sound in the palette menu.

Where your notes are kept

Everything is a plain file in your notes folder. Until you choose another, SideScoop keeps it in its own space on your Mac: ~/Library/Containers/com.sidescoop.app/Data/SideScoop. To use a different folder, choose Change Folder… in the menu bar menu.

Using a folder in iCloud Drive

A notes folder in iCloud Drive is backed up and can be used from your other Macs.

  1. Turn on iCloud Drive: System Settings › your name › iCloud › iCloud Drive.
  2. Click the SideScoop menu bar icon and choose Move to iCloud Drive….
  3. Pick or create a folder there. SideScoop copies your notes into it and uses it from then on.

On another Mac, choose Change Folder… and pick the same folder. A note iCloud hasn’t downloaded yet shows up once it arrives.

Using another Markdown editor

SideScoop’s files are ordinary Markdown, so you can open the same folder in any Markdown editor, or point SideScoop at a folder inside your existing notes. Choose Change Folder… and pick a folder of its own rather than the top of your notes, because every folder directly inside the notes folder becomes a tab.

  • Logs and Day are named by date (YYYY-MM-DD.md), the usual daily-note format. A line in Day is - 15:00 Call Mom, with 🔔 at the end for a bell.
  • Mind maps are .canvas files in the open JSON Canvas format.
  • A table is a Markdown table, or an HTML table once cells are merged, since Markdown can’t merge cells.
  • SideScoop shows pictures written as ordinary Markdown images and as ![[name.png]].

SideScoop reads the files again each time the panel opens, so changes made elsewhere show up.

The trial and SideScoop Pro

Click + and pick a panel marked Pro, or choose SideScoop Pro… in the menu bar menu, to see the Pro screen with Pro’s price.

  • Try Free for 14 Days starts the trial. Nothing is charged when it ends: Apple’s purchase sheet lists “14-day Trial” at no cost.
  • It lasts 14 days from when you start it. Reinstalling doesn’t restart it, and it can’t be started twice.
  • Then Pro tabs turn read-only: you can still open, copy, export and delete what’s in them, but you can’t change or add to them or make new ones. Another tab of a kind you already have needs Pro, but Day, Notes, Stickies, To-dos, Clips and Logs keep working in every tab, extra ones included. Day’s other days turn read-only, and new repeats and more than 3 bells a day need Pro; the repeats and bells you made keep coming. Clips holds 5, and Pro themes go back to Sketchbook. Nothing is deleted.
  • Buy Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks everything again for good. There’s no subscription.

SideScoop for iPhone

SideScoop for iPhone has the same tabs as the Mac, full screen, with the tab bar along the bottom. Tap + at the end of the tab bar for a new tab (swipe the tab bar sideways if it’s off screen). The gear button at the top of every tab holds the themes, the notes folder, Bell Sound, SideScoop Pro…, the Privacy Policy and this page. It needs iOS 15 or later.

Getting started: where your notes go

The first time you open SideScoop, it asks where to keep your notes:

  • Choose a Folder in iCloud Drive: pick or make a folder in Apple’s folder picker. Pick the folder SideScoop on your Mac uses, and both show the same notes.
  • Keep on This iPhone: notes stay in SideScoop’s own folder on the iPhone, which the Files app shows as On My iPhone › SideScoop.

The folder in use is named under Folder in the gear menu; tap its name to open it in the Files app. To switch, choose Change Folder…, or Keep on This iPhone Only to go back to SideScoop’s own folder. Nothing is copied or deleted when you switch: the folder you leave stays as it is.

Using the same notes on your Mac and iPhone

  1. On the Mac, click the SideScoop menu bar icon and choose Move to iCloud Drive… (see Using a folder in iCloud Drive), or Change Folder… if your notes are already there.
  2. On iPhone, choose that same folder, either on the first screen (Choose a Folder in iCloud Drive) or with the gear button › Change Folder….

iCloud Drive keeps the two in step. A change shows up once iCloud has brought it over, and SideScoop reads the folder again each time you come back to it. Each device keeps its own theme.

Writing a note

Tap the box at the top of Notes, or the + among the buttons at the top: the whole screen is yours to write in, titled with today’s date. Tap the date to give the note another title. Tap ✓ to put it away; if you didn’t write anything, no note is kept.

Undo

Tap twice on an empty spot in a tab to undo your last change there. While a message with Undo is showing, tapping twice does the same as its Undo button.

Bells and notifications

Day works as on the Mac: end what you type with /r, or tap the bell on a line. The first time there’s a bell to ring, iOS asks whether SideScoop may send notifications; it never asks at launch. Tap Allow. At that time a notification shows the time and what’s planned. If SideScoop is open, a banner shows inside it instead, with Snooze (10 minutes) and Done; a notification can be snoozed too (touch and hold it, then Snooze).

  • If you tapped Don’t Allow, Day says the bells are silent and offers a Settings button. Turn on notifications in Settings › SideScoop › Notifications (on iOS 18 and later, Settings › Apps › SideScoop › Notifications).
  • Bell Sound in the gear menu turns the sound of bells on or off on this iPhone.
  • SideScoop hands iOS the bells of the next 7 days. Open it at least once a week, so later bells, repeats included, are added too.
  • With the same Day folder on your Mac and iPhone, a bell rings on both.

Photos

To put a photo in a note, tap the photo button on the bar above the keyboard (right after the checkbox button) and pick one or take one. iOS asks before SideScoop uses the camera. Snippets and Doodles have a photo button at the top. Maps and Doodles have Save to Photos (the down-arrow button) in place of the Mac’s Export Image; iOS asks first, and SideScoop can only add pictures, not see your library.

SideScoop Pro on iPhone and Mac

SideScoop Pro and the 14-day trial belong to your Apple Account, not to one device: buy Pro or start the trial on your Mac or iPhone, and it works on the other too. The trial can be started once. If Pro doesn’t show up, tap the gear button › SideScoop Pro… › Restore Purchases. To use an offer code, tap Redeem Code… on the same screen.

Only on the Mac

Clips, the focus timer, Remind me tomorrow (the sun on a to-do), floating windows, the menu bar icon, the shortcut and the screen edge. A sun you set on the Mac still shows on the to-do on iPhone, and the reminder comes on the Mac.

Removing SideScoop from iPhone

If you chose Keep on This iPhone, copy your notes first: in the Files app, open On My iPhone and move or share the SideScoop folder. Then touch and hold the SideScoop icon and choose Remove App › Delete App. That removes its settings and the notes kept on the iPhone. A folder in iCloud Drive stays where it is.
