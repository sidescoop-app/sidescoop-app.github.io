SideScoop Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

In short: SideScoop doesn't collect anything about you.

  • What you make is saved as files on your Mac, in a folder you choose.
  • There's no account, analytics, ads, tracking or third-party code.
  • Nothing you write or copy is sent to me or anyone else.
  • Purchases go through Apple. I never see your name or payment details.

This policy covers SideScoop for Mac, made by Shourou Li, an independent developer (“I”, “me”).

Your notes

Everything you make in SideScoop (notes, stickies, to-dos, snippets, tables, logs, mind maps, drawings, trips) is saved as files in your notes folder. Until you choose another one, that's SideScoop's own folder on your Mac, ~/Library/Containers/com.sidescoop.app/Data/SideScoop. A to-do's reminder flag is saved in its file too.

If you put the folder in iCloud Drive, Apple syncs it under your own iCloud account and Apple's Privacy Policy. I have no access to it.

SideScoop also keeps settings on your Mac, such as your shortcut, theme, panel width, timer length, reminder time, how many clips to keep and where your notes folder is. Export Image saves a picture to your Downloads folder.

Clips

The Clips tab keeps the newest things you copy (text, links and pictures) so you can copy them again: 5, or with SideScoop Pro a number you choose from 1 to 500. When a new one comes in, the oldest beyond that number is removed.

  • It's off until you click Turn On in the tab, and starts with the next thing you copy. SideScoop reads your clipboard only while you have a Clips tab and Clips is on and not paused. A dot on the menu bar icon and on the tab shows when it's recording.
  • It skips anything the app you copied from marks as concealed or temporary, which is how password managers mark copied passwords. Not every app does this, so if you copy a password from somewhere else, delete it from the tab.
  • Limits: a text clip keeps its first 100,000 characters. A picture is scaled down to at most 2560 pixels on its long side, and one still over 20 MB isn't kept. With Pro, all pictures together stay under 500 MB; the oldest go first.
  • The history is kept only in SideScoop's own folder on your Mac, never in your notes folder. It isn't synced or sent anywhere. SideScoop shows it only in the Clips tab; nothing in your notes, and no website, can read it.
  • macOS may ask whether SideScoop may paste from other apps. You can change your answer in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Paste from Other Apps.
  • You can delete one clip, clear them all, or delete the Clips tab, which deletes the history and turns Clips off.

Keep moves a clip into Snippets (part of SideScoop Pro). From then on it's a file in your notes folder, like your notes. Snippets never reads the clipboard on its own.

The focus timer and reminders

When a focus session ends, SideScoop adds the minutes to that day's Logs file in your notes folder. The timer and the to-do reminder ask for no permissions and send no notifications.

When SideScoop goes online

If a note shows a picture by its web address, SideScoop loads it from that website when the note is shown, as a browser would. The website receives an ordinary request from your Mac, including its IP address, and handles it under its own privacy policy. This is the only time SideScoop goes online by itself, and it sends nothing else. Pictures you paste or drop in stay in your notes folder.

A link opens in your web browser only when you click it. Looking up a place or route in Trips opens a map website or the Maps app with the place names you typed, which then handles them under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

SideScoop Pro and the 7-day Trial are in-app purchases handled by Apple, and so are offer codes you redeem with Redeem Code…. I don't receive your name, email address or payment details. To unlock Pro, SideScoop asks the App Store on your Mac whether you've bought Pro or started the trial, and when. Apple's Privacy Policy covers your purchases, and Apple keeps your purchase history.

Crash reports from Apple

If you share analytics with app developers, Apple may send me crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you. Apple collects these, not SideScoop. To stop it, turn off “Share with app developers” in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Keeping and deleting your data

I don't hold any of your data, so there's nothing for me to keep, sell, share or delete. Everything SideScoop stores is on your Mac:

  • Delete notes and tabs in SideScoop (they go to the Trash) or in Finder.
  • To remove everything, quit SideScoop, delete the app, then delete ~/Library/Containers/com.sidescoop.app. That removes its settings, the Clips history and, if you never chose another folder, your notes.
  • A notes folder you chose yourself stays until you delete it.

Children

SideScoop doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If SideScoop ever handles information differently, I'll update this page and its date before that version is released. Any outside service it used would have to protect your data at least as well as this policy does.
