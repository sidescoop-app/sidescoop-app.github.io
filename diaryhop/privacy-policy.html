Diary Hop Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

In short: the developer collects nothing. Your notebooks live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, and only the people in a notebook can read it. There are no accounts of our own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Your notebooks

Everything you write, draw or add (pages, drawings, photos, Yearbook sheets, questions, covers) is kept on your iPhone and in your iCloud, through Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit). Diary Hop has no server of its own, and the developer can’t see any of it.

A shared notebook is shared through Apple’s iCloud sharing with the people who joined it. They see what is written in it, your name, your planet and its name. When you invite a friend by name, the letter travels inside a notebook you both are in, sealed so that only that friend’s phone can open it.

Photos you add are saved again as a plain picture, without their location or other camera data.

Pages from far away

A notebook written alone comes back from the wormhole with a page from another planet. These pages are written by the developer and come with the app. Nothing you write is ever sent to strangers.

Words that are starred out

Before anything leaves your iPhone, a short list of slurs and harsh swear words is starred out, on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere to be checked.

Invitation links

An invitation link opens a small page on sidescoop-app.github.io, hosted by GitHub, which hands the link to the app. The part of the link that opens the notebook stays on your iPhone and isn’t sent to that page’s server. GitHub may keep the usual records of a page visit; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Notifications

Notices such as “A package has arrived” are made on your iPhone. iCloud may wake the app when a shared notebook changes, so the notice can come on time.

Reports, hiding and contact

You can hide a person: their letters and pages no longer show for you, and they aren’t told. You can report a person, a notebook or a letter. A report is an email from your own mail app to the developer. It includes what the report is about (names and the notebook’s title), and whatever you write. It is used only to look into the report and answer you, and is deleted once it has been dealt with.

Purchases

Notebooks are bought through Apple’s App Store. The developer never sees your payment details or your Apple Account.

Keeping and deleting

The developer keeps nothing about you, so there is nothing to request. You can delete or leave notebooks in the app. Deleting Diary Hop removes what is on your iPhone; what is in your iCloud can be removed in Settings, under iCloud storage.

Children

The developer collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
