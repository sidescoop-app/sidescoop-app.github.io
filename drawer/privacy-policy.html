SideDrawer Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

In short: SideDrawer collects nothing. Your files, folders and settings stay on your Mac. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Your files

SideDrawer works on your files only on your Mac, in the folders you allow. Nothing is uploaded. SideDrawer keeps a bookmark to each allowed folder, in its own storage on your Mac, so it can open the folder next time.

Settings

Your settings (position, theme, pinned and recent folders) stay on your Mac.

The internet

SideDrawer doesn’t contact any server of its own. The only network traffic comes from:

  • web pictures in a Markdown file, which load when you view, print or make a PDF of it (no cookies are kept);
  • web links you click, which open in your browser;
  • Apple’s App Store, when you start the trial, buy, restore or redeem a code. SideDrawer never sees your payment details or Apple Account.

Keeping and deleting

SideDrawer has no servers and keeps nothing about you, so there is nothing to request or delete. Its settings and folder bookmarks stay on your Mac until you remove them: delete SideDrawer and the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.sidescoop.sidedrawer.

Children

SideDrawer collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
